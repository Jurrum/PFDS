Complete the pre-study questionnaire
– captures your current feelings about ordinary social-media feeds.

Land on your personal (empty) feed
– a blank timeline appears; no posts yet.

Prototype auto-generates 5 seed posts
– created on the spot via the OpenAI API.

Explore and “teach” the feed

Swipe → quick like / dislike

1–5 slider → precise rating after each swipe

Filter pills → switch topics or tap “+” to add a new category

(Optional) Drag posts up/down to re-rank or attach labels

Trigger fresh content
– every 5 interactions, the system appends 3 new posts (AI or subreddit source).

Check your stats (Dashboard button)
– view running counts of likes, dislikes, average rating, top categories.

End your session (End Session button on Dashboard)
– freezes logging, saves data.

Fill in the post-study questionnaire
– same 15-item scale + reflection questions about changes you noticed.

Session closes & data stored
– logs, ratings, and questionnaire answers are saved in a clean, anonymised report for the research team.
