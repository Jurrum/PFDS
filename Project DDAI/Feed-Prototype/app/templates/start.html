Welcome to the "Teaching the Feed" Study

Thank you for volunteering!
We are exploring how people shape—and are shaped by—personalised content feeds when they are given direct tools to "teach" the underlying algorithm.

What will happen

Pre-study questionnaire (≈ 3 min)

– asks about your current relationship with everyday social-media feeds.

Hands-on session (≈ 10 min)

– you will use an experimental feed prototype and interact with posts by swiping, rating (1–5), filtering categories, and adding new topics.

Post-study questionnaire (≈ 4 min)

– reflects on your experience with the prototype.

You may stop and withdraw at any point by clicking "Stop Session."

What data we collect

  • Actions inside the prototype (swipes, ratings, category changes, timestamps).
  • Your questionnaire responses.
  • No personal identifiers other than the username you create.

(Please choose a name that does not reveal your real identity.)

All data are stored securely, used only for academic research, and reported in aggregated or anonymised form.

Your rights

  • Participation is voluntary.
  • You may skip any question or end the session at any time without penalty.
  • You can request deletion of your data by emailing the research team (contact provided below) within 30 days.
