Welcome to the Feed Study

Instructions

This study aims to understand how people interact with social media feeds when given control over their content recommendation algorithm.

Your task is to:

  1. Create a username for this session
  2. Explore the feed interface and "teach" it what content you like
  3. Use features like swiping, rating, and category filtering
  4. Think about how the feed responds to your interactions

All your interactions will be logged for research purposes. You can end the session at any time using the "Stop" button in the dashboard.
